Practice 2: Objective Response Ranking

Objective

Demonstrate how to rank AI responses using consistent evaluation criteria rather than personal preference.

Exercise 1: Reading Benefits

User Prompt:

“Give me exactly two benefits of reading books. Use simple language and keep your answer brief.”

Response A:

“Reading improves vocabulary and helps people learn new things.”

Response B:

“Reading improves vocabulary, increases knowledge, strengthens concentration, develops imagination, and improves communication skills.”

Evaluation

Preferred Response: A

Response A follows the user’s instructions more closely because it:

* Provides exactly two benefits.
* Uses simple language.
* Keeps the answer brief.
* Addresses the user’s request directly.

Response B contains relevant information but provides five benefits instead of two. It also fails to respect the requested brevity.

Evaluation Criteria

Criterion	What to Check
Factual Accuracy	Is the information correct?
Relevance	Does it address the request?
Completeness	Does it include what was requested?
Clarity	Is it easy to understand?
Instruction-Following	Does it follow all explicit instructions?

Key Takeaways

1. More information does not automatically mean better quality.
2. Follow exact quantity requirements.
3. Respect the requested language and length.
4. Separate relevance from instruction-following.
5. Explain ranking decisions with clear evidence.

Conclusion

Objective response ranking requires evaluators to consider all relevant criteria and prioritize the user’s actual instructions. A response can contain relevant information but still rank lower because it does not follow the requested format or quantity.
