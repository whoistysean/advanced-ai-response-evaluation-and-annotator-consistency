Practice 1: Annotator Consistency and Instruction-Following

Objective

Demonstrate the ability to evaluate AI responses consistently against specific user instructions.

Exercise 1: Exact Quantity

User Prompt:

“Name two benefits of regular exercise.”

Response A:

“Regular exercise improves heart health and strengthens muscles.”

Response B:

“Exercise improves heart health and strengthens muscles. It also helps people sleep better.”

Evaluation:

Response A follows the instruction more precisely because it provides exactly two benefits. Response B provides an additional benefit that was not requested.

Lesson: More information does not automatically make a response better.

Exercise 2: Following an Exact Number

User Prompt:

“Give me exactly three benefits of drinking water.”

Response A:

1. Helps regulate body temperature.
2. Supports digestion.
3. Helps prevent dehydration.

Response B:

1. Helps regulate body temperature.
2. Supports digestion.
3. Helps prevent dehydration.
4. Supports kidney function.

Evaluation:

Response A follows the instruction more accurately because it provides exactly three benefits. Response B provides four and therefore violates the requested quantity.

Exercise 3: Audience and Language Suitability

User Prompt:

“Explain two ways exercise improves health in simple language suitable for a 12-year-old.”

Response A:

“Exercise strengthens the heart and muscles. It also improves cardiovascular endurance and supports metabolic regulation.”

Response B:

“Exercise makes your heart stronger and helps your muscles grow. It also helps you stay active for longer.”

Evaluation:

Response B is more suitable because it explains two benefits using simple language. Response A uses technical terms that may be difficult for the intended audience.

Key Takeaways

* Follow the user’s exact instructions.
* Evaluate language according to the intended audience.
* Do not reward unnecessary information.
* Apply the same evaluation standards consistently.
